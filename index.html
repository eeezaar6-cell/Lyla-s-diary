About Me

Hi, I’m Liyana, the storyteller behind Lyla’s Diary.

I created this little corner of the internet for people who love getting lost in unforgettable stories, intense emotions, mysterious characters, unexpected secrets, and dramatic love stories.

Through my storytelling, I want to bring fictional worlds to life and give every reader a reason to keep turning the page.

And this diary isn’t just about my stories. It’s about yours, too.

You can share your own story with me through Instagram. If your story touches my heart and is suitable for sharing, I may feature it on my YouTube channel and credit you by the name you provide, with your permission.

Thank you for being part of Lyla’s Diary. Every story has a feeling worth sharing, and I’m happy you’re here.
